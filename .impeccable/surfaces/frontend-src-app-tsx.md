## Direction contract

THESIS: Veil is a redaction desk you watch work, built as one pixel product: the document is cleaned in front of you line by line, and you approve it before anything is sent to an AI. It refuses stock web forms dressed with a pixel heading.

OWN-WORLD: Bitmap Bone v2. Bone ground #ecebe5, paper #f8f7f3, ink #0d0d0d, signal red #e2231a. Every control is pixel: 4px frames with notched corners drawn by border-image, raised buttons with a 4px pixel drop shadow that collapses when pressed, 2px chips and tabs, icons on a 12-pixel grid, document pictograms on a 16-pixel grid. Pixelify Sans for headings and interface text, Silkscreen for labels and numbers, Source Serif 4 only for the user's document, pasted text and the AI's answer.

STORY: The visitor drops a file, pastes text or opens a sample card, watches each personal value flash red and wipe to a black bar on a paper sheet, steps through the changes, fixes a miss or undoes one, approves, sees the knight win, then asks the AI and reads real names back.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Rail (wordmark, New document, session cards with pictogram and status chip, model status at the foot), then the intake: heading, one sentence, a dashed pixel drop zone beside a pixel paste field, then sample cards with a pixelated thumbnail of the real first page, type pictogram, pages and personal value counts.

FORM: Bitmap Bone, card A of the pixel round, rebuilt as a full component system on 24 October 2026 after the user's redesign brief; seed key 34f26c03. Signature interaction: the line-by-line redaction pass and the full-screen knight victory after Approve all.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
