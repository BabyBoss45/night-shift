# AI data leakage: verified numbers for the Veil pitch

Checked on 24 October 2026. Every number below was read on the page linked. Quotes are copied exactly and keep the source's US spelling. "Vendor" means the publisher sells a product that profits if the problem looks big.

Confidence key: high = primary source, clear method, large sample. Medium = primary source but vendor data, unclear base or no sample size. Low = press release only, method not shown, or a wording problem.

## 1. How people use ChatGPT (OpenAI and NBER)

Source for all rows: Chatterji, Cunningham, Deming, Hitzig, Ong, Shan, Wadman, "How People Use ChatGPT", NBER Working Paper 34255, September 2025. Page: https://www.nber.org/papers/w34255 . PDF read in full: https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w34255/w34255.pdf

Sample and method for all rows: about 1.1 million sampled conversations, 15 May 2024 to 26 June 2025, consumer plans only (Free, Plus, Pro). Messages classified by an automated LLM pipeline; no human read messages. Excludes users who opted out of training, users under 18, logged-out users, deleted chats, banned accounts. Business, Enterprise and Edu plans are not included.
