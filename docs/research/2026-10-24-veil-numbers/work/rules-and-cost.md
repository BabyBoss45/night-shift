# Veil: the rules and the cost of getting it wrong (UK first, then EU)

Checked on 24 October 2026. I opened every source below myself. Quotes are exact and under 25 words.

Two framing rules for the pitch:
- Veil keeps the key on the device, so its output is pseudonymised, not anonymised. For the user or firm it is still personal data.
- Never say Veil makes anyone "compliant". Say what the rule asks for, and how hiding values before they leave the device helps.
