# Veil: buyers, adoption, deals and market size

Checked on 24 October 2026. Every number below was read on the page linked in its row. Quotes are kept short and exact. Where a page shows a table, the quote is the table row. Deal values are in US dollars. Market sizes are estimates by research firms, not counts.

How to read the confidence column:
- high: regulator, statutory filing, official statistics or the company's own announcement, read on the page.
- medium: reputable survey or trade press, but with a vendor interest, a small or global sample, or a figure only seen in secondary coverage.
- low: weak source, unclear method, or a figure we could not trace to its owner.

What this means for the pitch:
- Mask-and-restore is now common. At least ten tools above already put real values back. Do not lead with it.
- The defensible claims are: learning each firm's own identifiers from corrections (only Private Guard and Mino show anything similar, both narrower), true redaction of PDFs as output, and UK-specific identifiers.
- Filed prices are lower than press-reported prices. Prompt Security, Lakera and Pangea all closed at about $160m to $225m in filings against $250m to $300m in the press. Quote the filed figure.
